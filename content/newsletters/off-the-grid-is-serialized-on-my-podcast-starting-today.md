Hey all, a lot of offline stuff happened recently. None of it good news, so I didn't have the energy to create anything new.

That being said, [I began serializing all my audiobooks onto my podcast for financial supporters](https://tips.pinecast.com/jar/weirdwritings) because I want to give everyone DRM Free ways to get the audiobooks while supporting me financially.

Off the Grid is the first book to be serialized as of today. [All my other audiobooks](/books) will follow.

[Off the Grid is now being serialized for Premium subscribers of my podcast.](https://tips.pinecast.com/jar/weirdwritings)

[You get a private RSS feed that goes into your podcast app of choice after you donate any amount here.](https://tips.pinecast.com/jar/weirdwritings)

[Pinecast explains how to use your private RSS feed here.](https://help.pinecast.com/article/61-subscribing-to-a-private-rss-feed)

[You can see public episodes here.](https://weirdwritings.pinecast.co/)

I'm serializing [my audiobooks](/books) to my podcast because I want to give people a DRM free way, and a pay what you can way, to get [my books.](/books). Audible isn't feasible for most of my readers so this is a way one can support me and still get access to my audiobooks.

I don't know when I'll create anything new again. For a while, you'll see older works of mine appear on the website. I believe these are important to still keep around to show how I've grown and changed, as well as preserve work that can no longer be found online.
