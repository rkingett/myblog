One of the things I love more than anything is community—and that could be community anything. Community marketing, community website building, I just love, love, community. that's why everything on this website is published under a Creative Commons license.

You are free to download, adapt, redistribute, remix, and share my work with anyone.

I especially would like my work to be syndicated in offline spaces because I am unable to access offline spaces because of my disabilities. I also want my work to be adapted for older technology because I cannot access those technologies because of my disabilities. Find some syndication ideas below but you are not limited to the ideas below.

## May I pay you once I've decided to syndicate your work?

You may pay me after you decide to syndicate my work. The original Creative Commons License forbids this kind of transaction but I'm poor. They don't know what it's like to live below the poverty line.

[You may work out a payment structure with me by contacting me,](/contact/) or [you may financially contribute in one of these ways.](/tip/)

## How would you like to be credited?

Ideally, with my full name but no middle initial. Robert Kingett. I’d also want to have my post URL fully cited in case someone wants to visit the URL for themselves. As a fallback, I’d like to have my full website cited so that way, people can visit my website to find the source of the reprint.

## Bio to include with publication.

Robert Kingett is a Blind and gay author with multiple disabilities. He writes fiction and nonfiction focusing on relationships. Robert is not on social media, so visit him online at https://sightlessscribbles.com/

## Syndication ideas.

You may redistribute [any of my posts](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1SGfMf1JAcEJw_Sg6n3QLCiUVot6crzpi?usp=sharing) beyond the below ideas.

* Put my audio files on a flash drive and give it to someone as a birthday gift.
* Print my work out and read it to someone offline.
* Read my works in full at an open mic event even though I am not there.
* Adapt my work into cassette tapes or Eight Tracks.
* Adapt my audio files onto records.
* Translate my work into any language including any form of sign language.
* Stream reactions to reading my work or hearing my work out loud.
* [Reprint my writings in an offline Zine.](https://archive.org/details/zines)
* Reprint my articles in your local newspaper.
* Reprint my articles, in full, on your own website or blog.
* Make fan recorded podcasts or video playlists reading or translating my work. For instance, into sign language.
* Print out my work and share it on the street.
* Broadcast my audio files on AM or FM Radio as well as Internet radio.
