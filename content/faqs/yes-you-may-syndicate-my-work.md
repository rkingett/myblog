This is to mainly answer the questions, [how to syndicate my work](/reprints/) and to answer some questions surrounding the respectability of syndication.

Let’s first answer the question, may you syndicate my work.

Yes. [You may syndicate my work.](/reprints) You may syndicate my work because that's combatting censorship, and I'm one hundred percent behind you in combatting censorship.

I can't stand censorship of any kind, no matter who is doing the censorship. Trying to erase racism from history is just as problematic to me as trying to prevent marginalized people from publishing modern works.

I fully believe one should have the liberty to read and interact with problematic art, even art that you consider harmful. I do not think problematic art should be willingly nor actively promoted beyond containment, but I strongly believe no one should dictate what kind of art one chooses to engage in.

[So, yes, you may syndicate my work far and wide. Fuck censorship to the highest order.](/reprints)

## May I pay you once I've decided to syndicate your work?

You may pay me after you decide to syndicate my work. The original Creative Commons License forbids this kind of transaction but I'm poor. They don't know what it's like to live below the poverty line.

[You may work out a payment structure with me by contacting me,](/contact/) or [you may financially contribute in one of these ways.](/tip/)

## How would you like to be credited?

Ideally, with my full name but no middle initial. Robert Kingett. I’d also want to have my post URL fully cited in case someone wants to visit the URL for themselves. As a fallback, I’d like to have my full website printed so that way, people can visit my website to find the source of the reprint.
